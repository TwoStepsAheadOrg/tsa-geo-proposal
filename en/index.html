The problem

Ask an AI for a recommendation. Your competitors come back.

Buyers now ask an assistant "who should I go with?" and narrow the field to the three to five names in that answer. Miss the list and you are out of the running — and most teams never find out it happened.

You cannot see which prompts drop you

A one-off check tells you almost nothing. Without repeated measurement you never learn which questions leave you out, or how often.

You cannot see why rivals get cited

If the documents behind the answer stay hidden, there is no way to decide what to publish next.

Your analytics never record it

People who discover you through an AI answer usually search your name afterwards. Analytics files that under direct or branded traffic, and the real discovery path disappears.

Frequently asked questions

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO is about ranking in a list of blue links. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is about whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, and similar engines mention, recommend, and cite your brand inside a generated answer. The scoreboard is not a rank position — it is share of voice, placement inside the answer, and which sources the model leaned on.

How is the data collected, and how often?

We run 48 crawling servers of our own and execute prompts against the live engines from IP addresses in each target country. Every registered prompt runs once a day on every selected platform, and we store the answer text, the brands mentioned, and the cited URLs as structured data.

Do you track Naver AI Briefing?

Yes. Naver AI Briefing sits in the same dashboard as the seven global platforms. Its citation mix is very different from ChatGPT's, so if you sell into Korea, reading both tracks together is the only way to get your priorities right.

How is the prediction accuracy validated?

On a holdout split of our proprietary 30M-record publication-to-citation dataset, the citation prediction model reached 8.6% mean absolute percentage error (MAPE). Error varies by industry, language, and prompt type, so we re-validate against your own data during onboarding.

Do you support our industry and language?

We collect multilingual prompts across 14 countries, so you can scope tracking by country and language. Industry is never a limitation — each account gets its own prompt set designed from scratch.

How long does it take to get started?

The free audit report arrives within 2–3 business days of your request. For a full rollout, once prompt design and competitor setup are done, daily data starts accumulating the following week.
